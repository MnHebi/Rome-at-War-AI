## 4. Known limits of this preparation

1. The 26 repaired sites not exercised in 508 (ids listed by the checker,
   e.g. 1042, 1174, 1181-1190, 1194, 1208, …) cannot be verified unless the new
   match reaches them; an unexercised call is not evidence of success.
2. The four `up-get-point-distance` point operands are free text, not traced
   sites, so they are covered by the error-family check plus the shipyard
   sampler diagnostics rather than by per-site invocation counts.
3. Shipyard placement/completion and the p7 migration failure boundary still need
   engine evidence; the checker reports their diagnostics but cannot prove
   gameplay success, and no improvement may be credited to the repair before
   that evidence exists.
4. A missing engine log, missing RAW58 records or a log without the repaired
   families exercised leaves the check inconclusive rather than passing.
